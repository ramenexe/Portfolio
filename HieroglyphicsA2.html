Hieroglyphics-"the words of God."

What are hieroglyphics?

Hieroglyphics are an ancient writing system that uses small pictures and symbols instead of standard letters. The word comes from Greek terms meaning "sacred carvings". Most famous in Ancient Egypt, these symbols were carved into temple walls, tombs, and monuments to record history, prayers, and stories

How do hieroglyphics work?

  • Mixed meanings: Symbols could represent actual objects, specific phonetic sounds like letters, or whole ideas
  • Reading Direction: They can be written left-to-right, right-to-left, or top-to-bottom. You look at the way the human or animal figures face to know which way to read.
  • Scribes: Only specially trained people called scribes knew how to write them. Everyday writing used simpler scripts like hieratic and demotic.

The Rosetta Stone

  1. Knowledge of the language was lost for over 1,500 years after Egypt's decline.
  2. In 1799, the Rosetta Stone was found. It had the same text in Greek, Demotic, and hieroglyphics.
  3. The stone provided the key to deciphering hieroglyphics by offering a parallel text in a known language.
